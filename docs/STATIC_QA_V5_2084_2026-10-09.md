1. The historical **64 changed source-pointer sites** compared with clean shipping ROM were rechecked against the exact v5-2084. The 8 previously repaired source-site references are still restored. Among 56 remaining differences:

A clean/v5 four-alignment exact pointer-like scan finds **270 patched string spans with some 32-bit values pointing into their rewritten interiors**, 262 with corresponding values in *clean shipping ROM*. These are **potential shared-suffix / overlapping-string hazards**, not yet proven live pointer sites. The 261 dynamic and 78 UI additions did not introduce any flagged span in this screening. Do not publish or continue mass-in-place writes until the 270 are triaged with source ownership.
3. **596** patched strings have at least one *encoded* line longer than 36 bytes; **1,132** longer than 30. Example `Route119_WeatherInstitute_2F_Text_ChangingWeatherRidiculous` reaches **108 bytes in one line**. This is a line-layout risk, not a pixel-width test; visual font glyph widths and renderer behavior need separate QA.
4. The inferred v0.4 Vietnamese codebook has **7 duplicate-byte mappings** (including `f`/`ấ`, `w`/`ằ`, `z`/`ắ`), explaining why untranslated English can display malformed glyphs. This is a codepage collision, not necessarily a bad byte write, but shows that partial English remains unsafe to display under the patched font.
